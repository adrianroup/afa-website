What Are Cookies

Cookies are small text files placed on your device when you visit a website. They allow the site to recognise your device on future visits and remember certain information — such as your preferences or whether you have consented to cookies.

How We Use Cookies

We use cookies on anaudiencefromanywhere.com for the following purposes. You can control which categories are active using the cookie banner or the preferences panel below.

Google Consent Mode v2

We have implemented Google Consent Mode v2. Until you accept Analytics or Advertising cookies, Google Analytics runs in cookieless / modelled mode — no identifiable data is collected and no cookies are set by Google on your device. Your choice is respected immediately.

Australian Visitors

We operate in Australia and are aware of our obligations under the Privacy Act 1988 (Cth) and the Australian Privacy Principles (APPs). We do not sell personal data. Advertising cookies are only set with your explicit consent. You may withdraw consent at any time using the preferences panel below.

EU & UK Visitors

We comply with the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) and the UK GDPR. Our legal basis for analytics and advertising cookies is your explicit consent (Article 6(1)(a) GDPR). You have the right to withdraw consent at any time — use the preferences panel below.

How Long Cookies Last

Necessary cookies are session-based and expire when you close your browser. Analytics and advertising cookies may persist for up to 13 months, consistent with Google's data retention settings.

Changing or Withdrawing Consent

You can change your cookie preferences at any time by clicking the button below or the "Cookie Settings" link in the site footer.
